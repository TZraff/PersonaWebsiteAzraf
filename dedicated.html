Close your eyes. That's when you can see it.

3D HORROR - UNITY - SINGLE PLAYER - IN DEVELOPMENT

CONCEPT

Behind The Closed Eyes is a 3D horror game
built around one simple idea:

“If you open your eyes, you can't see it.”

Something is haunting you, but it can only
be seen when your eyes are closed.

The ghost has five different states:

1. Roam
The ghost roams around the map until it detects the player.

2. Chase
When the ghost detects the player, it enters the Chase state and starts pursuing them.

3. Threat
When the ghost gets close enough to catch the player, it enters the Threat state. A short timer gives the player a final chance to escape before the ghost attacks. If the player manages to escape during this state, the ghost enters the Reaching state. However, if the player fails to escape before the timer runs out, the ghost immediately enters the Attack state.

4. Reaching
After the player successfully escapes the Threat state, the ghost enters the Reaching state. The ghost becomes more aggressive, gaining increased movement speed and a wider attack range. This state makes future encounters more dangerous and significantly reduces the player's chances of escaping again.

5. Attack
When the ghost successfully catches the player, it enters the Attack state, causing the player to die.
